Traefik Manager for umbrelOS

A community app store with Traefik Manager and the Traefik Manager agent.

Setup is needed outside umbrelOS. umbrelOS keeps ports 80 and 443 for itself, so the Traefik in these apps listens on other ports. Nothing outside your network reaches it until you forward ports on your router. Read the required setup before installing.

Add the app store

  1. In umbrelOS, open the App Store.
  2. Open the menu in the top right corner and choose Community App Stores.
  3. Paste this address and click Add:
    https://github.com/chr0nzz/tm-UmbrelOS
  4. Open Traefik Manager App Store and install the app you want.
